## Rules for future records

1. A national-team selection, tournament participation, match appearance, medal, and team-official appointment are different record types. Store them distinctly.
2. Do not infer an appearance from squad selection alone where an actual match cap is required.
3. Preserve the country represented. CUT athletes may represent South Africa or another country (for example, Botswana).
4. Keep pre-CUT national appearances, appearances made while enrolled at CUT, and post-graduation alumni achievements distinguishable.
5. Keep FISU/Universiade and USSA/CUCSA events distinct: USSA/CUCSA selection is not automatically a FISU World University Games appearance.
6. Require a credible source URL and human approval before public display. Never invent caps, dates, medals or competition IDs.
7. Use the existing player profile where it exists. Do not create duplicate athlete profiles just to attach an international record.
8. Do not infer that a player represented South Africa solely because they competed in an international club league or because they were named in a CUT Varsity team.
